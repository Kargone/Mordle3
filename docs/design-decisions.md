# Design decisions

These were picked from mockups in the visual companion on 2026-11-13.

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | Overall direction | **Midnight Ink**: navy `#0f1424` background, teal `#2bb5a0` = correct, amber `#f2a541` = present, slate = absent. Fraunces 900 wordmark ("Mor**d**le" with an accent-colored d) and DM Sans for UI. |
| 2 | Tiles | **Soft + tinted**: extra-rounded, borderless tiles. Scored tiles are a see-through tint of the status color with a bright letter in the same color. |
| 3 | Keyboard | **Pill keys in a raised tray** with a small bottom lip. Key colors follow the tile style. Enter and backspace are icons. |
| 4 | Header | **Centered heading** (help on the left, wordmark in the center, stats and settings on the right) with a **mode chip** under the wordmark that opens a menu. **One accent color per mode**: Daily is teal, Random is amber, InfiniMode is lavender `#a78bfa`. |
| 5 | Results | **Bottom sheet**: result headline and answer tiles, then stats, guess distribution, Share + Play Random, and the next-daily countdown. |
| 6 | Motion | **Juicy**: springy tilted letter pop, overshooting flip reveal, wobbly shake, win jump with sparkles. Everything is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. |

Share emoji follow the palette: 🟦 correct, 🟧 present, ⬛ absent.
