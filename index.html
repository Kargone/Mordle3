How to play

Guess the five-letter word. Each guess has to be a real word. After you guess, the tiles change color to show how close you were.

W is in the word and in the right spot.

T is in the word, but in a different spot.

O isn't in the word at all.

Modes

  • Daily: one word a day, the same for everyone. You get 6 guesses.
  • Random: a random word, or enter a seed to share a word with friends.
  • InfiniMode: unlimited guesses. Keep going until you get it.

Tip: hold ⌫ (or press Ctrl+Backspace) to clear the whole word.
